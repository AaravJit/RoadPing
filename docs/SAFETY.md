# RoadPing — Safety & Community Guidelines

_Last updated: 2026-05-28_

This is the canonical safety policy. The in-app screen at `app/safety.tsx`
mirrors this text.

## Drive first. Talk second.

Use RoadPing only when it is safe and legal to do so. If looking at your phone
would distract you, do not look at your phone.

- Hold to talk only when it is safe to do so.
- Mount your phone — do not hold it while driving.
- Pull over if you need to focus on the app.
- Obey all traffic laws and local rules of the road.

## What RoadPing is not

- **Not** emergency services. If there is an emergency, call your local
  emergency number.
- **Not** a way to report crimes — contact local authorities.
- **Not** turn-by-turn navigation or a safety-critical driving system.
- **Not** a tool for racing, evasion, or distracted driving.

## Community rules

Voice and any user-generated content in RoadPing must follow these rules.
Breaking them can lead to your account being suspended or permanently removed.

- No harassment, threats, or hate speech.
- No sexual or sexually suggestive content.
- No content encouraging reckless or illegal driving.
- No impersonation or doxxing.
- No spam or commercial solicitation.

## Block and report

From any driver's card in Nearby or in any room you can:

- **Block** — mutual: you both become invisible to each other.
- **Report** — silent: the reported user is never told.

We review reports and act on community-safety violations. Serious or repeat
violations can result in account removal.

## Privacy is on by default

- You are invisible until you tap Start RoadPing.
- Other drivers never see your exact coordinates.
- Hide makes you disappear from the map immediately.
- Voice is live only — nothing is recorded.
- No location history is stored.
