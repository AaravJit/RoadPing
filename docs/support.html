Frequently asked questions

How do I appear on the map?

You are invisible by default. Open the Drive screen and tap Start RoadPing to go live within your chosen range. Tap Stop or Hide at any time to disappear immediately.

Why does RoadPing ask for location?

Location is used only while RoadPing is active, to center your map and show nearby drivers. RoadPing requests "While Using the App" access only, never background location, and stores no location history. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy › Location Services.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The microphone is used only while you hold the talk button, and voice is delivered live to nearby drivers. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transcribed.

How do I block or report someone?

From any driver's card you can Block (you both become invisible to each other) or Report (silent — the other person is never told). We review reports and act on community-safety violations.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings › Delete Account in the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. If you need help, email support@roadping.app.

How do I add or change my vehicle?

Open Settings › Manage Vehicle. Choose your make, model, year, and color — RoadPing sets the right vehicle icon for your live map automatically.

Stay safe

Use RoadPing only when it is safe and legal to do so. Mount your phone, obey all traffic laws, and pull over if you need to focus. RoadPing is not navigation, not an emergency service, and not a tool for distracted driving. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
