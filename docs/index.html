Go live when you drive.

RoadPing is a live, map-first voice app for nearby drivers. You stay invisible until you tap Start — and Stop or Hide is always one tap away.

Designed for iPhone

What RoadPing does

You control live mode

Nothing is shared until you tap Start RoadPing. You are invisible by default and disappear instantly with Stop or Hide.

A map built around you

Your map stays centered on your drive. Nearby live drivers are listed with a broad distance range, never pinned on the map.

Hold to talk

Press and hold to talk live to nearby drivers. Voice is live only — it is never recorded, stored, or transcribed.

Private by design

Other drivers never see your exact location, no location history is stored, and Private Zones hide you near places you choose.

Drive first. Talk second.

Use RoadPing only when it is safe and legal to do so. Mount your phone, obey all traffic laws, and pull over if you need to focus on the app. RoadPing is not navigation or an emergency service.
